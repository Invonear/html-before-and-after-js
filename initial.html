A two-color print run

When you print a second color, the paper has to go back in exactly the same spot. Two strips of cardboard make a simple guide.

Line up the paper · Add the second color

Line up the paper

Lay a sheet on the table. Tape the strips to the table, one along the top edge of the paper and one along the left. Slide each fresh sheet into that corner before you print.

Add the second color

Print the lighter ink first. When it’s dry, slide a test sheet back into the corner and print the darker layer. Check where the colors meet before you run the rest.
