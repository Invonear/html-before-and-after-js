A two-color print run

Two scraps of card can save a whole stack of crooked prints. Use them to line up the paper for a second color.

Line up the paper · Add the second color

Line up the paper

Lay a sheet on the table. Tape one strip of card along its top edge and another along the left. Slide each fresh sheet into that corner before you print.

Add the second color

Print the lighter ink first. When it’s dry, slide a test sheet back into the corner and print the darker layer. Check where the colors meet before you run the rest.
