Where to find the difference in your browser

Open each example in its own tab. Choose View Page Source and search for “Print the lighter ink first.” You’ll find it only in the HTML version. With JavaScript on, open the Elements panel in your browser’s developer tools. There you can find the sentence on both pages.

What does this mean for SEO and GEO?

For search engine optimization (SEO), look at the links. A crawler can follow the links in the first article as soon as it gets the HTML. On the second page, they appear only after the script runs.

For generative engine optimization (GEO), look for the answer. Which ink goes on first? A reader that skips JavaScript can find “the lighter ink” in the first page’s HTML. Try the same check with a price or a delivery date on your own site.
